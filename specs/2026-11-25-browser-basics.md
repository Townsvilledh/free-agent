Goal
Prove general browser control on a live public site: open the Wikipedia article for Lagos, read it, follow ONE link from the article to a related page, extract one concrete fact about Lagos, save it, and screenshot the article page.

Hard rules
- Work only on en.wikipedia.org (authorised below). Do not open any other domain.
- The fact must come from text the pages actually showed — never invent it.
- Write the fact to fact.txt in the repo (path repo/fact.txt), one or two sentences, naming the fact plainly (e.g. a population figure or a named landmark and what the page says about it).
- Save the screenshot with browser_screenshot name repo/wiki.png while the Lagos article page is on screen.

Acceptance criteria
- browser_open succeeds on en.wikipedia.org (live browser control on a public site)
- The extracted fact about Lagos is written to fact.txt
- A screenshot of the article page is saved as wiki.png

Deliverables
- fact.txt with one concrete fact about Lagos, from the article
- wiki.png screenshot of the article page

Browser domains
- en.wikipedia.org
