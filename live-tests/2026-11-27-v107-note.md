## What shipped

Born of Ethuram's 2026-11-27 11:18 order ("the whole text should
not be selectable… handle it like Muse's own chat menu") and
the 11:28 screenshot exchange (pre-1.0.6 build: a handle
suggestion answered with an intake list while the X lane ran,
then a flat denial that she has an email or password of her own,
then "Want me to take that on as a task?" for "create an email
for yourself").

1. **No text selection in chat, at all.** The Select item is
   REMOVED from the message menu (MessageActions.ITEMS is now
   Reply / Copy / Share — reactions row unchanged), and its
   handler — the only `setTextIsSelectable(true)` in the chat
   screen — is deleted. No code path reachable from chat
   enables selection on a bubble; ChatBubbleSelectionTest was
   rewritten to pin exactly that at the source (the string
   appears nowhere in MainActivity). Settings / Wallet / Login
   layouts keep their selectable fields — addresses and keys
   are copy-targets there; the order was about the chat.
   Deliberate contract changes, pins updated: MessageActionsTest,
   Lille106Test's menu-contract pin, ChatBubbleSelectionTest.

2. **Identity-ask, explainer framing.** The guard's
   identity-ask detector is widened structurally (frames, not
   phrases): a DEPENDENCE claim — progress waits on, is paused
   for want of, or continues once given her identity data —
   in request, explainer, table or conditional mood is the
   same hit, as a set ("waiting for the … details", "details
   you … to use", "once I have those inputs", "let me know
   the missing details", each requiring identity items named
   in the prose) or as a single item under a giving/having
   clause ("once I have your email…", "paused until you give
   me the phone number", "waiting for you to provide the
   password"). The pasted blockers-table reply is the pinned
   negative fixture on BOTH the chat and report verdicts.
   Waiting on the world ("waiting for the confirmation email
   to land in her mailbox") names no details-set and no
   giving clause and still passes; the identity FACTS
   positives stay green.

3. **Self-denial verdict.** A flat claim that she lacks her
   own identity resources — "I don't have a personal email
   address or password of my own… I would need you to
   provide…" — is its own blocking hit (`identity-denial`) on
   chat + report paths: first-person non-possession bound to
   an identity item + an own-marker, and the
   need-you-to-provide frame over an identity intake. On a
   second hit the grounded DENIAL_LINE lands, asserting the
   CapabilitySheet's kit facts (handle + mailbox set up on
   the phone; password self-generated into the encrypted
   vault), and the regeneration re-asserts the sheet. The
   screenshot paragraph is the pinned fixture; non-denials
   ("I don't have YOUR email address saved") pass.

4. **Needs/blockers probe (deterministic).** In a chat whose
   newest work is active, paused, resting or parked, "how
   can I help / what do you need / what's blocking you /
   what do you need from me" is answered from the task
   record with zero brain call (StatusProbe.isNeedsProbe /
   needsTaskFor / needsAnswerFor): state + step, what she
   does next on her own, and the ONLY thing that can ever
   need the user — the human check in the browser panel,
   named when parked at one (an approval word or a browser
   hand-back when the record says exactly that). Closed-class
   grammar in the probe family's style (offer-of-help /
   her-need-asked-of-her / in-her-way frames); open-class
   objects break the parse — "what do you need for the
   report" is NOT intercepted, and neither is the user's own
   "I need help". Every composed answer is pinned guard- and
   narration-clean.

5. **Steering, not interviews.** In a packaged lane's chat
   (the X lanes, recognised by title), a message about the
   work's conduct — corrections ("email and password should
   be handled by you end to end"), suggestions ("for your
   username try …"), directions — is a STEER: recorded on
   the task record through the v1.0.4 pendingSteers queue
   plus a steer event, and answered from the record by the
   pure TaskSteer — what the lane is doing (ALREADY RUNNING
   stated when it is; the screenshot exchange happened while
   the browser chip was up and the brain narrated as if
   nothing had started) and what it uses (her own mailbox
   setup, her self-generated vault password). Never a brain
   intake interview. Messages with no conduct reference flow
   on exactly as before; the general-task steer path is
   untouched.

6. **The confirmation question is retired.** JobDetect's
   ambiguous band is taken on automatically (step 3 returns
   StartTask; "create an email for yourself" is a pinned job
   fixture); the reply is the one-line start ack that already
   existed. The chat system prompt now FORBIDS "Want me to
   take that on as a task?" by its exact wording, and the
   guard rejects the question as a reply (whole-prose shape,
   reason `confirmation-question`; quoting it mid-sentence
   passes). JobDetect.QUESTION and the pending-instruction
   resolution remain ONLY so a legacy thread's "yes" still
   completes honestly. Deliberate contract changes, pins
   updated: JobDetectTest (ambiguous fixtures + medium brief
   now expect StartTask), ChatHonestyPromptTest (prompt pin
   inverted to the forbidding frame).

## Honest limits

- Everything new is JVM/CI-proven; the first on-device
  exercise is Ethuram's install walk. In particular the
  long-press behaviour (no selection anywhere in chat) is
  source-pinned — a JVM test cannot instantiate a TextView —
  and proven only when he holds a bubble on the phone.
- The steer interception covers the packaged (X) lanes by
  title. A conduct message in a GENERAL task's chat still
  records a v1.0.4 steer AND gets a brain reply — the
  record-grounded steer answer is packaged-lane-only in
  this build.
- The needs probe gates on the chat's NEWEST record (the
  house latest-record rule): if the newest work finished
  while an older record sits parked, the question is not
  intercepted.
- The dependence/denial detectors are frame patterns, not
  phrase lists, and can in principle miss a novel framing;
  report-path withholding still errs on the safe side
  (reports recompose from the record).
- Kill/resume whole-message discipline is unchanged from
  1.0.6 (verb must lead).
