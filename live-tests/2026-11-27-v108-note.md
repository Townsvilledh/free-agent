## What was wrong (all proven on-device, 1.0.7)

- The X creation record read "Finished — Done" after
  three mailbox steps (emailnator → a tap →
  guerrillamail), verified by nothing but the browser
  resting on an allowlisted page. No account existed.
- The same expansion printed the record's stale
  "Waiting — continues automatically" note under the
  Finished headline.
- His follow-ups — "so the x profile is created?",
  "why did you claim its complete", "so where does
  the task stand now" — were not probe shapes, so the
  BRAIN answered: it disowned the card, claimed "I
  can't verify an active task's current status from
  this chat", and invented a confession. The record
  was readable the whole time; the probe ("what's the
  status") answered it correctly.
- The Connect-my-X lane parked correctly and he
  signed in with his own account in seconds — then
  "already logged in" got "I can't tell from here
  whether you're logged in." The browser's signed-in
  identity lived on no record; she could not tell his
  account from hers.
- The creation lane free-drove: no phase was ever
  stamped complete, so every resume re-wandered the
  mailbox ladder (37 steps of circles across runs).

## What shipped

1. **STATE SHEET on every chat turn** (`StateSheet`):
   a deterministic fact block composed fresh per turn
   from the live stores — each task's standing (state
   line, last named step, last movement), the
   browser's resting page, the identity stamps, her X
   account's verified standing, the accounts registry,
   the wallet families — riding every chat system
   prompt after the CapabilitySheet, never cached.
2. **THE STATE-QUESTION FAMILY routes to the record**
   (StatusProbe, structural closed-class grammar with
   per-record referents — no phrase lists):
   completion yes/no ("so the x profile is
   created?", "did it finish?"), accountability
   ("why did you claim its complete" — answered from
   what the record said and whether it stands),
   standing, the browser's page, and the LOGIN family
   ("already logged in", "am I signed in") answered
   from the identity stamp. Zero brain call whenever
   the chat has a relevant record. A finished packaged
   record is cross-checked against its finish gate
   inside the answer itself. Job-shaped lookalikes
   keep their non-interception pins.
3. **FINISH GATE for the creation lane**
   (`FinishGate`): Finished only when a live X
   session as her handle AND her profile page seen
   loading as hers evaluate true from state (a
   fresher session stamp naming anyone else
   contradicts the handle). A finish attempt without
   criteria → WAITING with the unmet criterion named,
   work continues. AUDIT-ON-OPEN: a Finished record
   with criteria false reopens to WAITING with the
   discrepancy + an audit event stamped and the false
   summary retired into that event — the current
   false-finished record heals on his next open.
   Deliberate contract change: the state machine
   gains ONE exit from COMPLETED (→ WAITING_ROUTE),
   audit-only; isTerminal is unchanged.
   Connect lanes are NOT gated, by design: their
   reports already grade a handle-less finish as NOT
   CONNECTED, so a false finish cannot stand there;
   identity stamps and probe cross-checks cover their
   truth. No other packaged lane exists today.
4. **STATE COHERENCE** (`StateCoherence`): a note is
   echoed only when it belongs to the record's
   current state — a waiting note can never print
   under a terminal headline again. Pinned as a sweep
   across all ten states over statusText,
   expandedText, and the graded report.
5. **STATUS-DENIAL guard verdict**: first-person
   inability + epistemic verb + own-state object
   ("I can't tell from here whether you're logged
   in", "I still can't verify whether the profile
   was created or the task continued afterward", and
   reworded variants) is blocked like
   capability-denial; the fallback is the record's own
   answer, discrepancy and identity stamp included.
   World-state denials ("I can't verify the price")
   pass untouched.
6. **SESSION IDENTITY STAMP** (`SessionIdentity`):
   at every packaged-lane start/resume and after
   every handback, the lane reads the signed-in
   handle from the page's account UI (in place when
   already on x.com — a mid-signup flow page survives
   a resume) and stamps it, or explicit signed-out.
7. **HANDBACK VERIFICATION + ANNOUNCEMENT**: the
   handback's first act is verify-by-state; a
   deterministic line lands in the origin chat
   unprompted ("Signed in as @… — verified from the
   page. …continuing" / "Still signed out — the step
   is still yours"), once per handback.
8. **ACCOUNT SEPARATION + IDENTITY PRECONDITION**:
   the creation lane names a foreign session in work
   terms ("the browser is signed in as @<him> — your
   account, not hers") and takes the sign-out branch;
   her handle is settled by vault_record_account and
   a foreign session never stamps xHandle on her
   record. Before any account-scoped action
   (x_post, x_read_feed, x_search) the live handle
   AND the registry's active entry must equal the
   task's target — mismatch stops and names it.
9. **ACCOUNTS REGISTRY** (`AccountsRegistry` +
   `AccountRegistryStore`): one durable entry per
   verified account {platform, handle, owner
   (lille|user), scope (hers autonomous-within-
   policy; his approval-gated), addedAt,
   lastVerifiedAt, lastActiveAt, state, active}.
   Registered only from a page-verified sign-in
   (never anonymous, never from chat); one active
   per platform; a switch is a recorded step (event
   + lastActiveAt). Visible in the state sheet, in
   chat answers ("which accounts do you have" is a
   deterministic family), and as a Settings →
   Accounts list in plain words. Rides the encrypted
   backup as METADATA only (`accountRegistry`
   payload field, merged on restore — the local
   active truth stands; no cookies, no credentials:
   her password stays in the vault, his are never
   stored anywhere).
10. **THE CREATION PHASE MACHINE**
    (`XCreationPhases`) + **LOOP BREAKER**
    (`LoopGuard`): MAILBOX → SIGNUP_FORM →
    EMAIL_VERIFY → PASSWORD_PROFILE → VERIFY_ACCOUNT
    (the gate), the human-check park as a conditional
    overlay that never resets a phase. Phase scripts
    post into the run once per phase entry; exits are
    stamped by the runner from evidence it can see
    (the committed address typed into the form; X's
    own code request; the code typed on X; the live
    session — later proofs cascade the checkpoints
    they imply). The current phase is DERIVED from
    the checkpoints: completed phases are never
    re-entered (mailbox ground past its phases is
    refused at browser_open) and a resume starts at
    the first incomplete phase by construction.
    MAILBOX COMMITMENT: one address, claimed via the
    new mailbox_claim tool (or auto-committed on
    first sight in the form), used for the whole run;
    mailbox_switch demands recorded evidence (X
    refused it — knowable only after a submit — or
    the code never arrived within the runner-counted
    poll budget of 6) and works at most once, reason
    on the record. The loop breaker counts browser
    steps on the same cluster (for the creation lane,
    the phase itself) without an advance, ON THE
    RECORD across rests and resumes: a trip takes
    the phase script's alternate branch (next
    provider) or parks with the loop named. The
    state sheet carries the lane's phase, its
    checkpoints, and the committed address.

the cluster key now normalises it. (3) The
status-denial pattern required "I can't" adjacent —

## Honest limits

- JVM/CI-proven only: no device in this environment.
  First on-device exercise is Ethuram's install walk.
  The proofs to watch: the false-finished X record
  REOPENS on open instead of sitting Finished;
  "so the x profile is created?" / "why did you
  claim its complete" / "already logged in" answered
  from the record with no brain involvement; a
  handback producing the unprompted verified
  announcement; the creation lane advancing phases
  instead of circling mailboxes.
- The identity read uses X's account-menu handle
  (the same read x_session_status already proved);
  a page shape X changes could make a read come back
  signed-out — the stamp then says signed-out, which
  is the page's testimony, and the lane re-reads at
  every start/resume/handback.
- Phase exits are evidence-stamped by the runner
  from tool calls and page text it can see; a
  completion it cannot see (e.g. X changes its code
  prompt wording) stamps late via the cascade from
  the live session rather than never.
- The loop breaker's thresholds (8 same-cluster /
  12 total no-advance steps) are tuned against the
  pinned circles; a legitimately long single-phase
  stretch parks with a named reason and resumes on
  his word — a named stop, never a silent circle.
