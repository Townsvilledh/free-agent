## The attempts (all recorded — this is the honest part)

The video rung worked at tool level from the start (a direct
host-driven capture produced 7/7 frames and two live quotes before
the spec runs). Getting the *keyless brain* to drive it end to end
took seven spec attempts; each failure class drove a machinery fix,
now unit-tested:

1. **BLOCKED** — the model could not emit the 27-step capture list as
   nested JSON inside one tool call (Sentinel-denied, then a 1-step
   retry, then it gave up). → Fix: `steps_file` (write the steps flat
   with write_file; capture loads them).
2. **Killed at 43 min** — cards, steps file, a full successful
   capture and narration all completed, then the model looped
   re-capturing instead of assembling. → Fixes: copy-exact narrate /
   assemble templates in the spec + call discipline; assemble learned
   the friendly argument shapes (narration_dir/ids, blur_regions,
   hold→duration).
3. **BLOCKED in 5 steps** — capture Sentinel-denied twice over the
   steps file's location (task root vs `repo/`). → Fix: one shared
   `resolve_steps` (task-relative, then `repo/` fallback) used by
   both the Sentinel and the handler, with precise self-correctable
   error messages; the Sentinel now validates goto URLs inside steps
   files too.
4. **BLOCKED in 3 steps** — the model refused outright ("I cannot
   proceed further"); the finish guard correctly rejected its finish.
   No machinery fault; model variance. The spec's Goal now leads with
   the throwaway-wallet framing.
5. **BLOCKED (wrongly)** — the first loop segment actually completed
   capture + narration + assembly (an MP4 existed), then a route
   cooldown made run_spec restart the loop *cold*: the new segment
   re-ran the capture against the already-registered demo name,
   failed, and its empty trace graded the finished video BLOCKED.
   → Fix: run_spec now **resumes from the checkpoint snapshot** on a
   route wait (regression-tested) — completed steps and their
   evidence are never redone or lost.
6. **BLOCKED (correctly)** — evidence graded properly (capture 6/6,
   narration 9 scenes) but duration was **44.4s**, 0.6s under the
   floor. Root cause: ffmpeg `-shortest` truncated every narrated
   segment to the raw audio length, discarding scene holds — the
   total was exactly the sum of the narration files. → Fix: `-shortest`
   removed, audio padded with `apad` to the segment duration
   (regression-tested: a 3s hold with 1s audio now yields ≥2.8s).
7. **SHIPPED** — 54.8s, all criteria evidenced. (Two verify_video
   calls were Sentinel-denied mid-run — malformed args from the
   model's text-fallback dialect — the third succeeded; the finish
   guard held until real VIDEO_OK evidence existed.)
