## The one fix: chat bubbles are text-selectable

Reported by Ethuram from his phone, with screenshots: in
chat, neither his own messages nor Lille's replies could be
selected or copied — long-press did nothing.

**Root cause (confirmed in code):** every chat bubble is
built programmatically by `MainActivity.appendLine()` as a
bare `TextView(this)`, and a programmatic TextView is **not
text-selectable by default**. The views that always worked —
the login pairing-code block, the wallet address — are
layout-built and carry `android:textIsSelectable="true"` in
XML. Chat never got the equivalent line.

**Fix:** one line in the single bubble factory —
`bubble.setTextIsSelectable(true)` in `appendLine`. Every
bubble flows through that factory (stored-message replay,
the user's sends, Lille's replies, the live reply bubble,
queued bubbles with their badges, the "…thinking" /
honest-waiting status bubble), so one line covers them all:
long-press → select → Copy now works on any chat text.
Nothing else about the bubbles changed — same layout
parameters, same backgrounds, same text; selection lives
inside the bubble and does not touch the Send→Stop morph,
the mid-flight queue, scrolling, or the composer. Long
replies still render in full (wrap-content height, unchanged)
and remain selectable along their whole length.

**Pinned by test:** `ChatBubbleSelectionTest` (2 tests). A
plain JVM test cannot instantiate a TextView, so the test
pins the contract at the source: it extracts the `appendLine`
body from `MainActivity.kt` and requires
`setTextIsSelectable(true)` inside it, and separately
requires the login layout's pairing-code block to keep its
`android:textIsSelectable="true"` (verified present and
untouched — no regression to the surface that already
worked).
